# Dhumbal privacy policy

Dhumbal is a card game. This policy covers the Dhumbal browser extension and its game server.

## Playing against bots

Nothing leaves your browser. Your game, settings and sound preference are saved only in the
extension's local storage on your computer.

## Playing with friends online

When you create or join an online table, the extension connects to the Dhumbal game server
(hosted on Cloudflare) and sends:

- the name you type, so the other players at your table can see who you are;
- a random player ID the extension generates, so you can reconnect to your table;
- your moves in the game.

The server uses this only to run the game at your table. The other players at your table see
your name and the cards you play; nobody else does. A table and everything about it is deleted
automatically after it has been idle for a day. Cloudflare, which runs the server, may keep
standard request logs.

Dhumbal has no accounts and no ads, does not sell or share data, and does not collect anything
else about you or your browsing.
